# Codex: Qualified Buyer Research

Use Codex's available logged-in browser or Computer Use surface for read-only search, post/profile inspection, and draft composer staging.

- Verify the account before opening a composer.
- Build or load the current user's versioned Offer Context Map before searching; do not inherit another user's niche or offer assumptions.
- Ask for 3-5 approved voice samples. With fewer than three, use the plain-human fallback and record low confidence.
- Search problem, failed-attempt, stakes, desired-outcome, then workaround/objection language. Check five results per query and stop after one candidate, 10 rewrites, or 50 inspected results.
- Require situation-first audience fit, a concrete current problem, one supporting signal, and desired-outcome fit before staging. Solution awareness is not required.
- Use scores to rank public-ready candidates and gate later stages, never to veto public-reply eligibility. Calls and offers require permission and commercial evidence.
- Do not reject a candidate merely because they sell something; reject seller funnels, contradictory profiles, and off-offer problems.
- Route genuine off-offer pain to adjacent research rather than the active Buyer Language Bank.
- Keep a short preamble visible before browser work.
- Stop if the browser exposes the wrong account, an unsafe thread, missing context, or an unavailable target.
- When staging is requested, type the exact approved draft into the destination composer and stop before send.
- Keep the first public reply offer-free. Clear context gets one mechanism, one action, and an honest tradeoff; an evidence gap gets one diagnostic A-C-A question.
- Preserve staged and sent variants and classify owner edits for message-policy learning only.
- Read back the composer text and record `staged_only`; never translate a visible draft into a sent claim.
- If the browser is unavailable, return the review packet and mark `fallback_used: true`.
