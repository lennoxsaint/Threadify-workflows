# Generic MCP: Qualified Buyer Research

1. Load the workflow manifest and reference policy.
2. Call `get_connection_defaults` and verify the intended account separately in the browser.
3. Use approved Brain context only when available; otherwise ask for audience situation, current problem, desired outcome, failed approaches, fit evidence, exclusions, later-stage commercial criteria, proof, and buyer-language inputs. Record them as a versioned Offer Context Map.
4. Ask for 3-5 approved voice samples. With fewer than three, use a plain-human fallback and record low confidence.
5. Search problem, failed-attempt, stakes, desired-outcome, then workaround/objection language. Check five results at a time. Stop after one qualified candidate by default, 10 rewrites, or 50 inspected results.
6. Inspect through the client's browser capability or use provided public URLs. Confirm the candidate authored a concrete current problem and inspect the full thread, profile, and relevant recent content.
7. Evaluate situation, problem, one supporting signal, and desired-outcome fit before scoring. Solution awareness is not required. Genuine off-offer pain goes to adjacent research.
8. Use scores to rank public-ready candidates and gate later stages. Calls and offers also require permission and commercial evidence.
9. Draft through the adaptive offer-free router and prepare a review packet with query run, Offer and Voice Context versions, evidence, stage, and reasons.
10. Do not call generation or reply-send tools excluded from the public workflow.
11. If composer staging is supported, type the exact draft and stop before send.
12. Emit the workflow receipt with source, policy, context, query run, evidence, variants, approval, composer, send, learning, and fallback states.
