# Safety and approval

A request to draft is not permission to import sources, schedule, publish or
share feedback. Match each action to the user's actual request and the current
tool contract. Do not infer approval from silence, a reminder or a prior batch.

## Review before delivery

Show workflow, intended account, exact copy/media, source links, adaptation mode,
gaps, proposed time/timezone and action. Bind approval to that display. Changed
cards need new approval; unchanged approved cards remain intact.

Validate and refresh facts, offers, sources, account/timezone and calendar
conflicts before scheduling, including later upfront drafts. Never overwrite
occupied slots. Persist pending attempts before dispatch, preserve successes
and reconcile unknown results before retrying.

## Supported service calls

Entitled `generate_content` may draft with Brain context. Authorized
`save_draft` stores exact copy. Neither action schedules or publishes.
Literal templates require evidenced rights and verified deterministic fills.
`save_final_draft` is audit-only, not editable storage.

Imports require approval of the selected sources and shared-Viral disclosure.
Scheduling, cancellation and rescheduling require exact applicable approval.
Feedback sharing requires its own exact-content opt-in. Brain writes require
approval of the exact memory changes. Native reminders require user intent and
configuration readback; they prepare reviews and never grant sending authority.

## Excluded public actions

Core workflows do not call `publish_now`, `send_reply` or
`enable_auto_reply`. QBR stages only. X artifacts are not X delivery proof.
Never bypass entitlements, credentials, security prompts or owner release gates.

Disconnected work remains local. Local draft, validation, approval, scheduled,
published and observed are different states. No receipt-shaped fixture proves
a provider operation, and directional observations do not prove caused growth.
