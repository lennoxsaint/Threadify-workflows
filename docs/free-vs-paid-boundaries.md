# Free methods and hosted services

The public MIT package includes the generalized creator method, Node engine,
schemas, tests, review controls and local drafting guidance. A Threadify account
is not required to work from user-supplied sources and confirmed facts.

## Public and local

- Guided source selection, coverage gaps and rights checks.
- Day, seven-day Week and 28-day Month blueprints.
- Host-authored drafts, exact review and manual continuation.
- Private state, interrupted-run recovery and duplicate-attempt prevention.
- Local feedback capture, with separate opt-in before sharing.
- Approval-gated orchestration of available connected services.

## Hosted in Threadify

Brain personalization, the shared corpus, proprietary ranking, generation,
saved drafts, calendar execution and analytics remain hosted services. Access
depends on the connected account's current entitlements; this repository does
not define or change plan prices or caps. Do not promise access from a manifest.

Use `generate_content` for entitled Brain-informed drafting. Use `save_draft`
to store exact host-authored or literal-template copy when connected and
authorized. `save_final_draft` records audit evidence only; it is not editable
draft storage. These calls do not expose the private implementation publicly.

If a service or entitlement is unavailable, continue local drafting and name
the missing capability. Never imitate a Brain result or saved/provider receipt.
A source being public or saved does not grant rights to copy it.

Legacy `paid_capability_stubs` manifest fields preserve compatibility. They
describe hosted boundaries, not a blanket ban on authorized service calls or a
promise that every listed capability currently exists.
