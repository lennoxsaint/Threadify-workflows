# Mission format

`MISSION.md` lives in the creator's private folder. It is the same file, in the same shape, that Threads Teach uses, so one mission serves both skills. The shape is adapted from Matt Pocock's `teach` skill - see [attribution](ATTRIBUTION.md).

One mission per folder. Keep it under a screen.

```md
# Mission: Threads

Status: draft | confirmed

## Why
{1-3 sentences. What changes for the creator if Threads works. A real outcome, not "get better at Threads".}

## What I sell or want
{The offer, or the thing they want people to do after finding them. "Nothing yet" is a valid answer.}

## Who it is for
{One real person, described plainly.}

## Success looks like
- {Something you could see happen}

## Limits
- {Time per week, topics they will not post about, anything else that bounds the work}

## Not now
- {Things the creator does not want to chase yet}
```

The setup questions, one at a time:

- Why are you on Threads? What changes for you if it works?
- What do you sell, or what do you want people to do after they find you?
- Who is it for? Describe one real person.
- How much time can you give this each week?
- What timezone are you in?

Rules:

- Write the file after the first answer with `Status: draft` and fill it in as the setup goes. Leave unanswered parts as `not asked yet`.
- Set `Status: confirmed` only after the creator has read the whole mission back and said yes.
- Never replace an existing mission without asking. If the goal has moved, confirm the change with the creator and update the file.
- Save the timezone in `NOTES.md` in the same folder.
