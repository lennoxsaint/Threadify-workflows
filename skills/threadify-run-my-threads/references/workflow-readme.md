# Run My Threads

"I don't write posts anymore. I approve them."

Use the complete [installable skill](../../skills/threadify-run-my-threads/SKILL.md).

Set it up once: connect Threadify, confirm your account and timezone, pick your CTA level and offer, and let your agent create one daily schedule (05:30 local by default). Setup builds a week plan and shows it as a table: five posts a day, a long-form thread on six days (teacher, storyteller, synthesizer in turn), one of your own greatest hits reposted word for word each day, and a proven structure and hook for every other post.

Every morning the agent asks Threadify for that day's posts, checks every hook, fits the posts into open slots on your calendar, and shows them in the agent thread. Reply "yes" from your phone and they are scheduled exactly as shown; reply "skip 2" or "no" to change that.

CTA levels: Growth (no CTAs), Balanced (two a day, 14 a week, the default) and Conversion (25 a week). A CTA is a thread with an Auto Plug reply or a list post whose offer lives in the second post, never in the first.

Threadify writes the posts with Claude Opus 5.5 when your plan allows it, Gemini as fallback. Your agent never rewrites them. The only change it may make is lowercasing (keeping names, brands and places), and a bundled guard script proves that for every post before you see it. Greatest-hit reposts are saved byte for byte, skipped if posted in the last 60 days or if a fact in them has gone stale.

Most always-on agents run on a pile of schedules, burn tokens and do very little. Run My Threads runs once a day, never touches posts already on your calendar, never publishes immediately, and reports what each run used when your agent's host shows it. A scheduled post is not a published post.
