If nothing is pending and I ask for examples, show them as historical filter examples, not send targets. For each shortlisted person, inspect their current public Threads profile in my authenticated browser and capture URL plus timestamp. Classify at most five people as Ready, Research, or Reject. If no warm person is Ready, switch to Qualified Buyer Research and use current public browser evidence for up to five candidates. Create the Lead Desk artifact and start its local HTML review server so I can edit the one proposed public reply and click Mark Final for Review. After I tell you to send, read the persisted exact final text and revision, verify the account, target, pending state and publishing gate, then send through an available delivery tool and check the live reply. The HTML itself never sends. Do not scrape, search automatically, DM, schedule, or claim a lead, booking, sale, or revenue result. If a required capability is unavailable, say so.

Before filming, demonstrate the tool scan and verified account on camera. Keep real names, private messages, and personal data out of the recording unless the person has given permission.
