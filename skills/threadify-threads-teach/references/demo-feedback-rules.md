# Demo feedback rules

Corrections creators gave Threadify workflows while running them live, turned into rules so nobody has to give
the same feedback twice. Every workflow follows these. A private overlay at `~/.threadify/owner-overlay.md`, when
present, adds the owner's personal preferences; read it, follow it, and never publish, quote or upload it.

## Pace and confirmations
- Ask each question once. Once the owner has answered or approved something, never ask for it again in the same run.
- Don't stop to report progress or ask "should I continue?" between steps the owner already asked for. Keep going to
  the next approval gate.
- Fix what you can yourself (a retry, a missing folder, a page that needs another fetch) before asking.

## Output on screen
- Lead with the answer, then the evidence. One short line before any long list.
- Keep chat phone-readable: no wide tables; highlight the few rows that matter.
- Show real numbers with their date and source. Show the thing being discussed; don't describe it.

## Truth and numbers
- Every number comes from a tool, a script or a source the owner can open. Unknown stays unknown, never 0.
- No hedging words in owner-facing copy ("might", "maybe", "could"). If evidence can't back a claim, cut the claim.

## Copy
- Threadify writes the copy. Never retype, paraphrase or "improve" it unless the owner asks in their own words.

## Failure handling
- When a step fails, say exactly what failed and what you did about it in one line, then continue or stop cleanly.
