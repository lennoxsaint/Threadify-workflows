# 8. Replying on your own posts

## Principle

A reply is a conversation with one person that other people can read. Treat it that way.

- **Read the whole exchange first** - your post, their comment and anything in between.
- **Keep it short.** Usually one sentence. Add a second only for a specific detail, a real callback or a useful question.
- **Match their energy.** Talk like a person answering a person. Do not turn their comment into a slogan.
- **Help with the question first.** If they asked something, answer it in public.
- **Ask before you pitch.** Someone being curious or kind has not asked to buy. Only treat it as buying interest when they say they want to buy, join or talk privately, and ask permission before you send a link or move it to messages.
- **Only say what is true.** No invented experience and no invented facts about what you sell.
- **Skip what should be skipped.** Abuse, spam, pile-ons, and anything touching health, legal trouble or a crisis.

## Worked example

An example written for this lesson, not a real exchange. Swap in your own true details.

Comment: "how long before you saw any traction?"

Weak reply:

> great question! consistency is key

Better reply:

> about four months before anything moved - the first three felt like talking to a wall. what month are you on?

It answers the question, has a real detail and gives them a reason to reply.

## Common mistake

The reply that could sit under any comment. "great point", "so true", "love this". If it would work anywhere, it adds nothing here.

## On the learner's posts

This skill does not read or send replies. Ask the learner to paste two or three comments from their own posts, with the reply they gave if there was one. Say that you can only see what they paste.

## Practice task

Take three real comments from your posts. Write a one-sentence reply to each that could only belong under that comment.

## Lesson post

Write a post that invites a real answer - a question you actually want answered, with enough context that people can answer it well. Plan to reply to the first comments yourself, by hand.

## Edge of the free material

This lesson teaches how to write a good reply. Handling replies at volume is outside it. Use the one-line pointer if the learner asks.
