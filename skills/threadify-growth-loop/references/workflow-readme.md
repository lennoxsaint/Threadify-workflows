# Threadify Growth Loop

Threadify Growth Loop is the evidence layer on top of the 30-Day Viral Vault. It reviews mature post outcomes, keeps a private hypothesis ledger, changes only tomorrow's two challenger slots, and saves six drafts. Four proven slots remain stable. It never schedules or publishes.

## Daily contract

- Sources: two Greatest Hits, two Viral Vault, two My Vault.
- Roles: four proven, two challenger.
- Topics: two broad, two expertise, two personal.
- Structures: at least three, including one listicle.
- CTA: zero or one earned CTA tied to the verified offer.
- Learning: a challenger changes one named dimension and binds one explicit reviewed hypothesis. A new user starts with two owner-reviewed candidates; mature evidence re-ranks them and marks only repeat-tested winners promoted.

A hypothesis is promoted after at least three comparable mature tests across at least two publication days, at least two positive results, a median result above its matched baseline, no safety or rights issue, and no known attributed commercial regression. Promotion is a proof label, not a cold-start prerequisite. Missing post-level attribution remains unknown; it is not manufactured into evidence.

## Runtime contract

The host agent checks for newly mature 72-hour and 7-day observations every six hours. It records each post/checkpoint once, prepares the next six-card day, persists a save intent before each provider call, and reconciles an authoritative draft readback. Replayed evidence is idempotent; conflicting evidence blocks.

Use `threadify-workflows growth-loop status --state /absolute/private-directory` for body-free state, `display-hypotheses` for the ledger, and `display-run --date YYYY-MM-DD` for the day and save receipts. `pause` and `resume` require the current revision. The state directory must be private and POSIX owner-only.

Persistent registration belongs to the host. Follow the bundled setup-adapter guide, obtain approval before registering a native or operating-system job, verify one real invocation, and retain the actual job ID, next run, device requirement, and pause command. CLI presence alone is not runtime proof.

## Safety boundary

The workflow may automatically save drafts only after the owner approves that standing scope during setup. There is intentionally no schedule or publish command. Scheduling one challenger for a demo remains a separate owner-approved 30-Day Viral Vault action with exact account, copy, and time shown before the call and read back afterward.
