# AI Content Forensics

AI Content Forensics turns one inspectable creator corpus into evidence-linked
content rules and an opinionated ten-post thread. It works from local exports by
default. A host may also supply read-only Threadify results or a bounded public
sample from Scrape Creators.

## Contract

1. Confirm the target creator, destination platform, corpus sources, rights, and
   desired comparison metric.
2. Normalize every source into the bundled corpus schema. Keep platform metrics
   separate and retain observation time, provider, coverage, and evidence IDs.
3. Deduplicate by platform/native ID and then by creator plus normalized body.
4. Use comparative performance language only for a platform/format family with
   at least 20 unique metric-valid items. Otherwise return descriptive patterns.
5. Select seven distinct findings. Every number and claim must name evidence in
   the normalized corpus or a deterministic aggregate emitted by the engine.
6. Produce exactly ten posts: hook; seven numbered findings, each followed by a
   `copy this:` template; one action post; and one CTA.
7. Return the constitutions, insight audit, final thread, optional evidence-bound
   visuals, and a body-free receipt. The result is a private draft, never proof
   of causation, future performance, provider save, schedule, or publication.

## Collection routes

- **Local export or URL manifest:** preferred and available without an account.
- **Threadify:** use only current read capabilities for an explicitly verified
  owned account. Missing tools or entitlement route back to a local export.
- **Scrape Creators:** optional BYO-key read path. Keep the key in the environment,
  cap the sample, record vendor/freshness/cache state, and delete or retain raw
  responses according to the user's chosen private storage policy.

Do not ingest private messages, customer material, private profiles, demographic
enrichment, login cookies, or an unbounded audience. Do not redistribute whole
third-party corpora, transcripts, or media in workflow bundles or public receipts.
