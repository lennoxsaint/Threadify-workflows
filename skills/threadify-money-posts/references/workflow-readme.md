# Money Posts

"Stop chasing views."

Use the complete [installable skill](../../skills/threadify-money-posts/SKILL.md).

Money Posts ranks your last 90 days of Threads posts by one number: **clicks per 1,000 views** (unique clicks on your tracked links, divided by views, times 1,000). A post with 800 views and 50 clicks beats a post with 100,000 views and 20 clicks, and the table shows it. It also lists your biggest posts that carried no tracked link.

Your agent then explains the 2-4 patterns your top click posts share, each tied to the posts that show it. Threadify writes three new posts in that pattern, each with its own tracked Auto Plug for your saved offer, and your agent fits them into open slots over the next three days. You see one approval packet (account, timezone, the three exact posts, the three exact plugs and destination, the three times). Nothing is scheduled until you say "yes", and nothing is ever published immediately.

Your agent never writes or edits the posts. The only change it may make is lowercasing (keeping names, brands and places), and a bundled guard proves `lower(original) == lower(final)` for every post and plug.

No click data yet? With fewer than 5 linked posts or fewer than 30 clicks in 90 days, Money Posts says so and ranks on proxy signals (replies per 1,000 views, and replies asking how or for the link), labelled "proxy, not clicks". Seven days after the new posts go out, run it again to rank them by real clicks.

Conversions are reported exactly as Threadify labels them, a click is not a sale, and a scheduled post is not a published post.
