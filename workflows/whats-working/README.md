# What's Working

"Threads isn't dead. Most of your posts are."

Use the complete [installable skill](../../skills/threadify-whats-working/SKILL.md).

Your views feel dead and you can't tell which posts Threads is burying. What's Working reads every post you published this year and measures each one against **your own median views**, so a 500-follower account and a 50,000-follower account read the same way:

- **dead**: under your median
- **stuck**: 1x to under 3x your median
- **alive**: 3x your median or more

It answers "is Threads dead for me?" month by month in plain numbers, shows what the dead posts share, and names the **#1 kind of post Threads still pushes** on your account, with its multiple and three of your real posts as proof. A local report page charts every post against your median.

Then Threadify writes three new posts in that #1 pattern, with three editable blocks and a Done editing button; optionally add tracked Auto Plugs for your saved offer, and your agent fits them into open slots over the next three days. You see one approval packet (account, timezone, the three exact edited posts and times, plus enabled plugs and their destination only when you choose them). Done editing saves your revisions and their comparisons; it does not approve scheduling. Auto Plugs start off and can be skipped without an offer lookup. Nothing is scheduled until you say "yes", and nothing is ever published immediately.

Threadify generates every post and plug, and the agent preserves that wording; requested lowercasing is proven with the casing guard. Posts under 3 days old are left out because they are still collecting views, missing numbers stay unknown, and if your account's history doesn't reach back to 1 January the workflow says the real window instead of "this year". Seven days after the new posts go out, run it again to see where they landed.

The editor preserves generated originals and your exact final words. Drafts use a concrete research hook, compact factual findings and a direct closing point in your own voice. The analysis keeps coverage and caveats. Research and drafting credits remain accurate, and medians stay labelled as medians.
