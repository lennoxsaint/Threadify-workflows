# Grill To Post

Use the installable skill `threadify-grill-to-post`; its `SKILL.md` holds the full instructions.

This workflow takes one rough idea and ends with three scheduled Threads posts. It first asks numbered questions, each with a recommended answer, until the reader, the point and the takeaway are clear. Then Threadify writes the idea three ways in the creator's voice: a thread, a list post and a one-liner. The agent shows those outputs unedited and does not write post copy itself.

Before anything is scheduled, every number in the drafts is checked against the creator's own account, and anything that cannot be matched is flagged for the creator to cut, soften or knowingly keep. The creator can pick from ranked first-line options for the thread.

Scheduling needs exact approval of one packet: the account, the three exact texts, each local date and time, and what auto-repost will do. Each post is scheduled once and the calendar is read back. A connection never grants that approval, and a scheduled post is not a published post. Without a connection the workflow stops at the confirmed brief and says so.

The workflow does not promise growth. It ends by pointing to `threadify-threads-teach` for lessons on how the posts are built.

The question method is adapted from Matt Pocock's [`grilling` skill](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling) under the MIT License; the notice ships with the skill in `references/ATTRIBUTION.md`. He did not write, review or endorse this workflow.
