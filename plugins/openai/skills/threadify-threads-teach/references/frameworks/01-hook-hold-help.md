# 1. Hook, hold, help

## Principle

A Threads post has three jobs, in order.

- **Hook** - the first line. It gives the reader a reason to stop scrolling.
- **Hold** - the middle. A scene, a detail or a bit of proof that keeps them reading.
- **Help** - the end. The reader leaves with something they can use or a clearer way to see their own situation.

A short post can do all three in three lines. A post that is missing one usually shows it: no hook and nobody reads it, no hold and nobody believes it, no help and nobody remembers it.

## Worked example

A real post by @lennox_saint, quoted exactly. Published 26 May 2026. 102,781 views when this lesson was written.

> Eminem dropped 300 songs. you replay 8.
> MrBeast uploaded 976 videos. you saw 12.
> Seinfeld wrote 1,000s of jokes. you quote 3.
> Kobe took 1m practice shots. you saw 1 ring.
> Stephen King published 65 novels. you read 2.
>
> volume is the strategy. create more.

Hook: the first line - a name everyone knows and a number that surprises. Hold: four more lines in the same pattern, each one adding weight. Help: the last line, which tells the reader what to do with it.

## Common mistake

Starting with the help. The post opens with advice ("post about one thing"), so there is no reason to keep reading and no reason to believe it.

## On the learner's posts

Connected: read their best posts with `greatest_hits` (`metric: "views"`) and their recent ones with `read_post_performance` (`include_full_text: true`). Follow the post-picking rules in the skill: best posts by views with the age and view floors, full text for recent posts, and the contrast-post rule. For the weak post, prefer one that opens with the help. Ask the learner to label hook, hold and help in one strong post and one weak post, then compare what is missing.

Not connected: do the same with posts they paste, and call them a sample.

## Practice task

Take one of your own posts that went nowhere. Label the three parts. Write down which part is missing or weakest, in one sentence.

## Lesson post

Write one short post with all three parts, on a topic from your mission. Mark where each part starts before you finalise it.
