# The curriculum

Ten short lessons. Each one teaches a single framework from the [framework notes](frameworks/), on the learner's own posts, and ends with one post they write.

| # | Lesson | The learner leaves able to | Framework note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hook, hold, help | Label the three parts of any post and write one with all three | [01](frameworks/01-hook-hold-help.md) |
| 2 | Openers | Write a first line that names what is happening | [02](frameworks/02-openers.md) |
| 3 | Pick the shape | Choose short, two-part, list or story to fit the goal | [03](frameworks/03-pick-the-shape.md) |
| 4 | The two-part post | Split one idea into a clean first post and a second beat | [04](frameworks/04-two-part-post.md) |
| 5 | Receipts | Back a claim with a real detail, or soften it | [05](frameworks/05-receipts.md) |
| 6 | Your voice | Rewrite a stiff post the way they would say it out loud | [06](frameworks/06-your-voice.md) |
| 7 | The pre-publish check | Run five questions on a draft before it goes out | [07](frameworks/07-pre-publish-check.md) |
| 8 | Replying on your own posts | Answer a comment so the conversation keeps going | [08](frameworks/08-replying-on-your-posts.md) |
| 9 | Commenting on other people's posts | Add something to someone else's post that fits the room | [09](frameworks/09-commenting-elsewhere.md) |
| 10 | Reading your own results | Turn last month's posts into one thing to try next | [10](frameworks/10-reading-your-results.md) |

## Order

Lesson 1 comes first for everyone. After that, pick the lesson that is the smallest useful step toward the mission, given the learning records.

- No posts yet, or posting rarely: 1, 2, 6, 7, then the rest.
- Posting often with little response: 1, 2, 3, 5, 10.
- Getting attention but no conversations or customers: 4, 8, 9, 10.

A learner who already knows a lesson can skip it. Record what they said they know, then check it with one retrieval question in a later session.

## Practice by retrieval

From the second session on, start with two or three questions about earlier lessons, answered from memory. Use the lesson's own framework note to write them. Good questions ask the learner to do the thing ("label the hook, hold and help in this post of yours"), not to recite it.

Bring back an older lesson every session, not just the last one. When the learner misses a question, teach that point again briefly and add a learning record.

## After lesson 10

Keep going on the learner's own posts: pick the weakest framework from the learning records, reread that lesson together, and write another post. The curriculum does not add new frameworks past this point. Say so with the one-line pointer in the skill.

## What these lessons do not cover

Ready-made hook lists, post-by-post breakdowns of other creators' viral posts, daily growth planning, follower attribution, experiment tracking, and paid course or community material are not in this skill. Do not make them up. If a learner asks for one, say it is outside the free lessons and use the one-line pointer.
