# Research Army: viral hook templates for the post step

Ten hook-and-structure templates that come up again and again in high-performing Threads posts and in Threadify Vaults.
The post step picks one so Threadify writes the research post on a proven shape. Fill `[brackets]` only with the
winner's checked facts; the source line still reads "the N most-liked posts from K keyword searches". These shapes are
proven for engagement in general, not yet for research posts in particular; say that if asked.

## Pick one

1. Prefer the owner's own winners. When Threadify is connected, call `greatest_hits` (and `list_vault_items` when the
   owner has a Vault) and look for a hook shape the owner already uses that fits this post. Name the shape, not the
   post: never quote another creator's post or handle.
2. Otherwise pick from the list below: the template whose hook best carries the single most surprising checked fact.
   Template 1 suits most research posts.
3. Say the pick in one line: "Template: <number and name>, because <the fact it carries>."

## The ten

1. **The pattern.** `a pattern i've noticed in [group]:` / `[surprising finding].` / 2-3 checked facts, one per line /
   `[encouraging so-what for the reader]`
2. **Popular opinion.** `popular opinion:` / `[claim the facts support].` / `not because [expected reason].` /
   `because [what the facts show].`
3. **You're still early.** `[old thing] is [age]. [new thing] is [age].` / the facts / `if you're reading this, you're still early.`
4. **Big vs small.** `[big name or group] did [big number]. you saw [small number].` (2-3 times, from the facts) /
   `[lesson]. [short command].`
5. **Receipts.** `i [did the work: pulled N posts from K searches].` / `[fact 1].` / `[fact 2].` / `[fact 3].` /
   `if you're [pain], here's your [relief]:`
6. **Unpopular opinion.** `unpopular opinion: "[common label]" just means [honest reframe from the facts].`
7. **My page is for you.** `if you're a [specific person] who's realised [truth the facts show]...` / the facts /
   `[encouraging so-what].`
8. **Small brave act.** `everyone says [advice].` / `i counted.` / `[fact that contradicts it].` / `[small action] is enough.`
9. **The gurus.** `[gurus] say "[common advice]".` / `no. look at [checked evidence].` / `[punchline].`
10. **Side by side.** `when [group a] posts, [observation from the facts].` / `when [group b] posts, [observation].` /
    `[so-what].`

## House rules for every template

- The first 3-5 words carry the surprise.
- Compact: one fact per line, about 300 characters or fewer.
- The last line is an encouraging so-what aimed at the reader: what the finding means they can do, said warmly
  (for example "your own story is enough. go tell it."). Never end on a statistic or a question.
- No links and no offer in the post; the offer goes in the Auto Plug.
