# Hook bank

Fill-in hook templates, grouped by the archetype ids in `threads-playbook.md`, for the agent to name in `inputText`.

Rule: Fill brackets only with true details from the creator's own context; a number only if it really happened.

- Pass a template as a shape for Threadify to follow, not as finished copy. Threadify writes the hook.
- If a bracket cannot be filled with something true, pick another template.
- Every filled hook must still pass the playbook's hook checks and banned-opener list.

## contrarian

- [Common belief] is wrong. Here's why:
- "[Advice everyone repeats]." Wrong. [What is true instead].
- Stop [popular habit]. Start [better habit].
- [Popular advice] works. Just not for [specific group].
- Everyone says [common advice]. I did the opposite.
- A few things I believe about [topic] that most [people in the niche] don't:
- [Thing people chase] is overrated. [Thing they ignore] isn't.

## confession

- I got [topic] wrong for [time].
- I [did the thing] for [time] before I admitted [truth].
- I almost [quit / gave up on / walked away from] [thing].
- Nobody knows this, but I [true private detail].
- I used to [old behaviour]. I'm not proud of it.
- Admit it: when you [common situation], you [common reaction]. I do too.
- I [did something embarrassing]. Here's what it taught me:

- [Surprising true fact, with its source]. Here's why that matters for [audience]:
- The part of [process] nobody talks about:
- I noticed something odd about [the creator's own posts / clients / work].
- One question changed how I [do the thing]:

- When I [old situation ended], I [new situation began]. It was [hard detail].
