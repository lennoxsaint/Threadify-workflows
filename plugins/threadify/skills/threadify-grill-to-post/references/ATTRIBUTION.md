# Attribution

## Question method

The way this skill questions an idea before anything is written is adapted from the `grilling` skill by Matt Pocock, which his `grill-me` skill runs:

https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling

Adapted from it: questioning in numbered rounds until a shared understanding is reached, a recommended answer on every question, looking facts up instead of asking for them, leaving every decision to the person, and not acting until they confirm.

Changed here: the subject is fixed to one Threads idea, the facts come from the creator's own posts, the questions end in a short brief, and the brief feeds an approval-gated generate, check and schedule sequence that his skill does not contain.

The mission file shape in [mission format](mission-format.md) is adapted from his `teach` skill in the same repository.

If you want to stress-test any other plan this way, use his original.

Matt Pocock did not write, review or endorse this skill.
